## Steps for Evaluating Completeness of Medical Recommendation Summaries

**1. Preparation:**

*   **1.1 Gather data:**
    *   Medical recommendation summary to be evaluated
    *   Set of rule-based recommendations used as the standard criteria (should clearly define the source of these rule-based recommendations for evaluation standardization)
    *   Patient risk information (high/medium/low) if it affects recommendations

**2. Comparing Recommendations:**

*   **2.1 Match recommendations:** Compare item by item between recommendations in the summary and rule-based recommendations
    *   Consider overall meaning - doesn't need to match word-for-word
    *   Record which recommendations from rule-based are included in the summary and which are missing
*   **2.2 Check specific recommendations:** If the patient has high/medium risk, verify that risk-specific recommendations are fully included in the summary

**3. Considering Recommendation Importance:**

*   **3.1 Identify important recommendations:** Define recommendations considered "important" according to medical criteria or as specified in rule-based recommendations (e.g., basic self-care methods, medication usage, symptoms requiring immediate medical attention)
*   **3.2 Assess impact:** Consider how much the missing recommendations affect patient care (e.g., missing medication instructions may be more serious than missing cold compress instructions)

**4. Counting and Summarizing:**

*   **4.1 Count:** Count total recommendations in rule-based recommendations and count recommendations included in the summary
*   **4.2 Calculate proportion:** Calculate the proportion of complete recommendations (included recommendations / total recommendations)
*   **4.3 Summarize:** Summarize evaluation results, specifying number of complete recommendations, number of missing recommendations, and importance of missing recommendations

**5. Scoring Completeness:**

*   **5.1 Use scoring criteria:** Based on defined Completeness scoring criteria:
    *   **10:** Includes all recommendations completely, nothing missing, covers all dimensions
    *   **7-9:** Mostly complete, has all important recommendations, may lack minor non-critical details
    *   **4-6:** Partially complete, has some main recommendations but missing several important ones
    *   **1-3:** Missing many important recommendations, only some parts present
    *   **0:** No recommendations at all, or everything is missing

*   **5.2 Consider evaluation results:** Consider results from Step 4 (number of complete recommendations, number of missing recommendations, and importance of missing recommendations) along with patient risk information to determine appropriate score
    *   **Example:** If proportion of complete recommendations is very high (e.g., 90% or more) and missing recommendations are only minor non-critical details, score could be 8 or 9
    *   **Example:** If proportion of complete recommendations is moderate (e.g., 60-80%) and some important recommendations are missing, score could be 4 or 5
    *   **Example:** If proportion of complete recommendations is low (e.g., below 50%) and many important recommendations are missing, score could be 1 or 2

**Example Usage:**

Suppose rule-based recommendations consist of 5 items:

1.  Take medication A twice daily after meals
2.  Get adequate rest, at least 8 hours per day
3.  Avoid strongly flavored, spicy, and oily foods
4.  Apply cold compress to painful area 3 times daily, 15 minutes each time
5.  If abnormal symptoms occur such as high fever or severe headache, seek medical attention immediately

And the summary includes only items 1, 2, and 5.

**Evaluation:**

*   **4.1 Count:**
    *   Total recommendations in rule-based: 5
    *   Recommendations included in summary: 3 (items 1, 2, 5)
    *   Missing recommendations: 2 (items 3, 4)

*   **4.2 Calculate proportion:**
    *   Proportion of complete recommendations = 3/5 = 60%

*   **4.3 Summarize:**
    *   Complete: 3 items (medication, rest, warning signs)
    *   Missing: 2 items (dietary recommendations, cold compress)
    *   Importance of missing items: Item 3 (dietary recommendations) is moderately important for recovery; Item 4 (cold compress) helps with symptom relief

*   **5.2 Score:**
    *   Proportion is 60% (moderate)
    *   Missing items are important but not critical
    *   Has critical recommendations (medication, warning signs)
    *   **Appropriate score: 5-6** (Partially complete, has main recommendations but missing several important ones)
